Help

Set up a plan, read the projections, and move your data.

Set up a college plan
  1. Open Plan. Choose Yes if you want to contribute a percentage of salary. Enter your investment balance, contributions, and expected return. Enter 5 for 5%.
  2. Add each child’s college dates and estimated expenses. Use the year of your cost estimate as the base year.
  3. Use Advanced options if you need to change projection years, track additional investments, or make changes in specific years.
  4. Review the projected balances and college costs in Overview.
How the projection works

The planner applies each investment’s return to its positive opening balance, then adds contributions and subtracts withdrawals. It pays college costs and any existing shortfall from the investments in the order they’re listed. Unfunded amounts carry forward without interest. The largest funding gap is the largest amount left unfunded at year end.

Student costs grow from each estimate’s base year and apply for the specified attendance years. A blank spouse salary increase uses the primary rate. With salary funding on, additional annual contributions grow at the primary salary increase rate unless you enter a separate contribution increase. With salary funding off, a blank contribution increase keeps that contribution amount flat.

The automatic horizon covers at least ten years and extends through the final attendance or adjustment year. A stop year is the first year without the associated pay or contribution. With salary funding on, the primary contribution stop year also stops additional annual contributions. Balance overrides and one-time transactions apply only to their year. Salary, recurring contribution, and rate changes carry forward.

Save, restore, or clear your plan

Your plan saves automatically to this browser profile. Use Data & backups to export before switching devices, clearing browser data, or replacing the plan with an import.

To move your data, copy the JSON backup to your other machine, open the planner there, and import the file. To bring over a plan from the original spreadsheet-based planner, enter its inputs on the Plan page.

After the planner loads online, supported browsers can open it offline from their cache. You can also install it from the browser menu when the browser offers that option.

Maintain annual defaults

The first year of a new blank plan follows your device calendar. The example assumptions are illustrative and maintained in the annual defaults module. Existing plans retain their saved years and assumptions.
