How to use My Pay · instructions & example
  1. Enter your salary, pay frequency, filing status, and state. Blank amounts count as zero. Type an exact amount or use the step buttons.
  2. Add benefits and select either percent of pay or dollars per paycheck for each retirement plan. Only the selected method counts; other entries are kept for switching back.
  3. Enter your federal W-4 elections. These control federal withholding, not your actual income. Enter dependents as listed on your W-4 or use its Step 3 dollar total.
  4. Choose state withholding: an estimate using regular wages, actual dollars per paycheck, or California DE 4. Extra state withholding is separate from federal Step 4(c).
  5. Inputs save on this device by tax year. Export a backup, or use Reset & clear saved inputs to remove them.

Example: adjusting your W-4

With $75,000 in salary and biweekly pay, enter $12,000 in Step 4(a) if that is the additional income on your W-4. This increases federal withholding without adding $12,000 to your pay.

Enter $50 in Step 4(c) to withhold another $50 from each paycheck. State withholding stays separate: use the state controls if you also want to change it. Step 3 credits reduce federal withholding. No year-end tax balance is added to your paycheck.

This is a single-job, full-year planner. For planning multiple jobs, itemizing, or sharing amounts across W-4s, use Easy W4. State estimates use the existing model and omit advanced rules, credit phaseouts, local taxes and SDI. They are not a completed tax return. W-4 instructions · Privacy and saved inputs
