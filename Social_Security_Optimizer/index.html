PUBLISHED FACTS, SEPARATE FROM YOUR PLAN

Annual data, made maintainable.

SSA reference tables are versioned independently. Updating them keeps your saved inputs intact.

Update each year

  1. Run Update-SSA.cmd in the project folder.
  2. Review the dated report and the SSA source links.
  3. Run Activate-SSA.cmd YEAR to activate the validated release.
  4. Reload the app. Your plan stays saved.

The updater stops if SSA is unreachable or the tables do not pass validation. The working data stays active. See the included annual-update guide for the saved-page fallback.

Official sources

    Published values versus projections

    Historical wage caps, national average wages, eligibility-year bend points and announced COLAs come from SSA. Unpublished future values use the growth assumptions in your plan. A new COLA does not replace your long-term COLA assumption.

    UNDERSTAND THE COMPARISON

    Know what is in your estimate.

    Benefit estimates

    Choose methods independently for you and your spouse. SSA mode accepts your own monthly amounts at ages 67 and 70 in today’s dollars, excluding spousal benefits. Enter the dollar year and birthday month/day. A spouse’s age-70 amount is optional; if blank, it is calculated from claiming credits. This mode compares ages 67 and 70 only, without inventing SSA quotes for other ages.

    Quick entry uses one year of Social Security-taxed earnings in $1,000 steps and your age on December 31 of that earnings year. Use a full work year as the reference and enter the stop-work age separately. The history method accepts exact covered earnings by year; only the Social Security-taxed column is used. Noncovered earnings are excluded. The highest 35 indexed years determine the benefit; missing earlier years count as zero. The primary history method can project later work; spouse history does not.

    Claiming and household income

    Retirement reductions use SSA's monthly reduction rates; delayed credits stop at age 70. The comparison searches whole-year claiming ages 62–70 from your current age onward for wage-based inputs, or ages 67 and 70 for entered SSA estimates. If you are over 70, your entered claiming age is held fixed. Selected ages in the past are treated as an existing claim.

    A spousal supplement begins only after both people have claimed, and is reduced when started before full retirement age. Pensions are shown separately and do not trigger WEP or GPO reductions.

    Timing and limitations

    Enter ages as of December 31 of the current year. Saved ages advance when the calendar year changes. The plan includes the entered planning-through age. SSA entries use the birthday month (the preceding month for a birthday on the first); wage-based claims use full-year equivalents. Amounts are nominal, before income tax, Medicare premiums, investment returns and discounting.

    Survivor estimates begin at the survivor's retirement full retirement age and use the larger own benefit or simplified deceased-worker benefit. Early survivor claims, survivor-specific retirement ages, switching strategies, disabled or divorced-spouse rules, family maximums, retroactive claims and eligibility-credit checks are not modeled.

    Working while claiming

    The earnings test approximates annual withholding before full retirement age, with a prorated FRA year. It does not model the first-year monthly test or the later increase in benefits for months withheld. If you plan to work while claiming early, the ranking is incomplete for that situation.

    Official figures support the estimate; SSA determines your actual eligibility and payment. The original spreadsheet export is retained separately for comparison.
