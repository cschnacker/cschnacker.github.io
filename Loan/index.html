How this planner calculates payments

The loan is amortized monthly: the annual interest rate divided by 12 is applied to the opening balance, then the monthly principal-and-interest payment reduces the balance. The final payment is capped at the amount due. Yearly totals summarize the same monthly schedule.

The first payment is in the selected month of the start year. Existing plans default to January. Annual extra principal and one-time payments apply after the December regular payment. Taxes and insurance are divided by 12 and increase each January after the start year. Modifications take effect each January. The projection stops at payoff or 100 years. Taxes and insurance after payoff and loan fees are not included.
