Privacy & your data

Updated October 6, 2026.

Easy W-4 calculates in your browser. This app has no accounts or server-side calculation. Calculator code does not send your entries to a server. Google AdSense may be enabled on approved deployments as described below.

Saved on your device

Calculator entries are saved in this site's local browser storage, separately by tax year. Anyone using the same browser profile may reopen them. Reset & clear saved inputs removes the active year's saved inputs. Clearing this site's browser data removes local saved data; private browsing may not retain it.

Files you choose to save

Export inputs downloads a JSON file containing your entries. Keep that file private. Import reads a file you select locally. Tax tables are bundled with the app and are updated through new releases.

Advertising

When enabled on an approved website or supported app frame, Google AdSense loads third-party advertising code. Google and its partners may receive information such as your IP address, browser or device information, and visits to this site, and may use cookies or similar storage to provide and measure ads. Our ad integration does not pass calculator inputs, results, or exported files to the ad request.

Where applicable, the deployment must provide Google's consent messages or another certified consent platform. Use the consent controls shown by that deployment to manage your choices. How Google uses information from sites and apps · Google ad preferences

Hosting and external links

Your hosting provider and app store may process ordinary connection or download information under their own policies. IRS and other external links open outside this app and follow those sites' policies.

For support or privacy requests, use the developer contact listed on the store listing. Calculator data stays on your device, so the developer cannot retrieve or delete it remotely.
