Understand your estimate

How to use the calculator

  1. Enter your taxable income after deductions.
  2. For a flat rate, enter one rate. For a progressive schedule, select Add bracket and enter the upper limits in increasing order.
  3. Enter a rate for every bracket, including the final bracket with no upper limit. You can use up to 14 brackets.
  4. Review your effective rate, total tax, and the amount taxed in each bracket.

What is the effective rate?

Total calculated tax divided by the taxable income you entered, multiplied by 100. At zero income, the calculator displays 0%. Calculations use full precision; displayed amounts are rounded to cents.

Your entries and offline access

Calculations run in your browser. Select Remember my entries to save them in this browser on this device. Clear entries removes that saved copy. After offline setup completes, you can reopen this app without a connection while its cached files remain available.

Install using your browser’s app installation or Add to Home Screen option. The Install app button appears when supported.

Calculation limits

This tool applies the bracket arithmetic you provide. It does not look up tax rules or calculate deductions, credits, withholding, refunds, or special tax treatments.
