Using the planner

Enter a starting scenario, add expected changes, and inspect the years that matter to you.

Set up your household

In Overview, choose a start year, projection length, your age, and the federal and state filing statuses. Enable a spouse to enter both sets of work and retirement income. For a separate return, enter only the income covered by that return. Choose Other to use your own effective income tax rates and turn off U.S. payroll taxes.

Enter income and retirement dates

Enter annual starting-year amounts and growth rates. The controls step by $1,000 for wages, pensions, and Social Security, and by $500 for other taxable income. You can also type an exact amount. Wages stop in the year the retirement age is reached. Retirement income begins at its specified age and uses the entered growth rate from the starting year. Pre-tax payroll deductions are capped at wages and reduce income taxes. Enter Social Security separately from taxable pension income.

Add holdings, dependents, and future changes

Holdings generate distributions from projected value times yield. For a future purchase, enter the price expected at first inclusion. Values compound after inclusion. Select ordinary, qualified, or tax-free distributions. The end age excludes the holding in that year. Appreciation is unrealized and distributions are paid in cash. A change takes effect at the start of its year and remains in effect. For two changes to the same field in the same year, the last entry applies.

Read the estimates

The annual chart stacks net income, federal income tax, state income tax, and payroll tax. Payroll tax combines Social Security and Medicare. Each band’s thickness shows that category’s amount. Negative net income falls below zero, and years with incomplete tax estimates appear as gaps. Hover over the chart to see all four amounts for any year. To see the same amounts in the legend and update the tax breakdown, use Inspect year.

Federal and state effective rates use gross cash income. Forecast years use the most recent available annual release and apply your indexing assumptions to bracket limits and deductions. Years before the first release use its amounts without indexing.

The model uses standard deductions and employee payroll taxes on entered wages before pre-tax deductions. It estimates nonrefundable dependent credits assuming otherwise eligible dependents and models qualified distributions at federal 0%, 15%, and 20% rates. Enter eligible earned income, education, and other credits as dollar estimates. State calculations use the shared filing-status mappings and workbook allowances, exclude Social Security benefits, and omit local taxes and state-specific adjustments. The planner also excludes itemizing, self-employment tax, refundable credits, AMT, NIIT, and special senior or dependent deductions. Use these figures for planning.

Move or erase a plan

Download a backup in Plan data, move the JSON file to the other machine, then choose it under Import plan and confirm the replacement. Use Clear plan to remove the browser’s copy. A downloaded backup still contains the data you exported.
