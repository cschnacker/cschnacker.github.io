Help & instructions

Getting started

Choose your start month and the month and year to plan through. The end month is included; the plan can span up to 50 years. The graph and table end earlier if all balances reach zero and stay there. Add a card, then enter its balance, APR, minimum payment rules, recurring new charges, and extra monthly payment. Enter APR as a percentage (for example, 24 for 24%). Values recalculate and save as you type. You can add up to 100 cards.

Future changes and annual increases

Annual increases apply to recurring charges and extra payments every 12 planning months. Under a card's scheduled changes, choose the month and year when the change should take effect. Enter only values you want to replace; blank means unchanged. A balance replacement applies before that month's interest. APR and recurring changes remain in effect; one-time charges and payments affect only the chosen month. Only one change row per month is allowed. Scheduled dates are relative to the plan start; shifting the start month shifts the scheduled dates.

Payoff calculator and locked payments

At the bottom of an expanded card, choose a payoff month and year and select Calculate payment. The calculator finds a fixed monthly payment that clears the projected balance by that date, including planned charges and interest. Apply payment to plan saves that amount and updates both graphs and the schedule. A locked amount replaces minimum-plus-extra calculations; minimums apply if higher and one-time payments remain additional. Unlock payment restores the retained extra-payment settings. The target must be within your plan. Changing balances, rates, charges, or dates can affect an existing lock; calculate again to update it. Later charges can create a new balance.

How the projection works

Interest = opening balance × APR ÷ 12. Minimum payment = the greater of your dollar minimum and your percentage of opening balance, capped at the amount owed. Extra and one-time payments are added and capped at the full amount owed. New charges are included this month and begin accruing interest next month. Values round to cents each month. Payoff means the balance reaches zero and stays zero through the end of the selected horizon; future charges may change this. No payments are transferred automatically between cards. Issuer fees, daily compounding, and promotional-rate rules are not modeled.

Saved data, backups, and moving machines

Your plan is saved in an IndexedDB database in this browser for this site. Check the save indicator. Export database downloads a versioned JSON file containing all your cards, settings, and scheduled changes. Move that file to another computer and use Import database there. Import replaces the current plan after confirmation. Browser data clearing, private browsing, changing the site address, or using a different browser can remove or separate saved data. Keep an exported backup. Backups contain readable financial amounts; store them privately. Do not enter account numbers.

Clear my data removes this app's saved plan and resets the interface after confirmation. Exported files remain on your computer. Avoid editing this plan in multiple tabs at once.

Updating for a new year

Maintainers update data/annual.json for the year and new-plan defaults. Existing plans keep their own settings and card values. Card rates and payment requirements are user inputs, not annually published rules. Update those from your own statements. Application releases and the user database are separate.
